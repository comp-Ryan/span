Two lanes

Fast lane
Event arrives (payment, reply, slider). Finance tool returns numbers in well under a second. Node updates immediately. Many calls per minute, no prose.
Slow lane
Debounced. Planner reads before/after numbers and context, decides levers, writes the spoken sentence and the report. Two to five seconds. The "why" lands after the "what" has already moved.
On the call
Gemini Live hears and reasons and calls tools in one loop; Jev (or the Owed model) answers the finance tools in under 300 ms; ElevenLabs speaks the reply in ~100 ms. About one second to the first spoken word. FINANCE_TOOLS=jev|owed_model is config.
